# Detection Scenario Platform (DSP)

**Generate realistic, observable security activity for XDR and NDR customer POC validation.**

DSP is a reusable POC platform for environments where there are too few meaningful alerts or cases to demonstrate product value. It discovers targets, runs safe detection-oriented scenarios, records structured events, and produces evidence that engineers can review and use in customer reporting.

<Note>
DSP validates **traffic and event generation**. It does not automatically claim that a vendor alert fired or that an XDR case was successfully correlated. Detection, alert, and case outcomes remain subject to the connected security platform and human verification.
</Note>

## Why DSP exists

Secure or isolated customer environments often produce too few useful alerts during a limited POC window. This creates three practical problems: there may be little proof to show in a clean network, customers still need screenshots and evidence for internal reporting, and generic BAS tools may generate activity that does not map cleanly to the detections and workflows the POC team wants to demonstrate.

DSP focuses on the missing operational layer: **repeatable, observable activity that can be mapped to detection and investigation workflows without turning the POC into a destructive attack exercise.**

  </Card>
  <Card title="Structured evidence" icon="file-lines">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Operator-friendly profiles" icon="sliders">
    Use `normal` for representative targets or `high` to expand the same per-target activity across all discovered targets.
  </Card>
